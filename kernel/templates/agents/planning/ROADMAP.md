## Rules

- Milestones describe intended outcomes, not progress. Do not annotate one with
  "in progress", "partially complete" or "blocked" — that is status, and status
  lives in `agents/RUN_STATE.md`.
- A roadmap edited only when the plan itself changes stays true. One edited to
  track progress is stale by the next session.
